# Tuning profiles

## Western reference

Minor-third reference:

`0.5 : 1.0 : 1.2 : 1.5 : 2.0`

Major-third variant:

`0.5 : 1.0 : 1.25 : 1.5 : 2.0`

These are Western bell references only.

## Chinese zhong

Chinese two-tone targets are not part of the production tuning profile set.

The previous neutral `1.20–1.25` engineering band is intentionally not retained as a historical Chinese target. The research canon records the minor/major-third references separately and requires the physical modal mechanism to be established before any interval becomes a production target.

Experimental Chinese target/evolution files, if needed by future researchers, are preserved under `research/chinese_bell/bells/` and are not consumed by the normal optimizer.
