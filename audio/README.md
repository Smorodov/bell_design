# Reference audio

`bell_round_q100.wav` is the retained circular reference rendering (`q=1`). It is a listening example, not a calibrated sound-pressure recording or an experimental reference measurement.

The adjacent `bell_round_q100._morphing.txt` records how the geometry-listening command generated this file. At `q=1` the coordinate map is the identity. For `q<1`, `synth-geometry` currently uses the documented reduced-order Rayleigh morphing estimate; it must not be interpreted as a fresh direct FEM solution.

To regenerate the standard modal listening sample from `bells/best_bell.dat`, run `make_audio.cmd`.

## Contact-driven directional example

`bell_contact.wav` is generated by `bell synth-contact` from `bells/best_geometry.json`. It uses the direct cylindrical vector-FEM Fourier basis m=0..3, a finite Hertz hammer contact at 72% profile height and azimuth 0°, damped modal free response, and a complex far-field surface-radiation integral for a listener at polar angle 30° from the bell axis and azimuth 0°. It is mono PCM16 at 48 kHz, 6 seconds, peak-normalized to 0.95. This is a directional model-based listening preview, not calibrated SPL, stereo, room acoustics, or a validated full acoustic solution. See `docs/CONTACT_DRIVEN_AUDIO_PIPELINE.md` and `audio/bell_contact_metadata.txt`.

Regenerate with `generate_contact_audio.cmd` after building the project.


## Internal clapper example (1.2.140)

`bell_clapper.wav` is generated by `bell synth-clapper` for the bundled geometry. It models a 5 kg illustrative rigid pendulum with 0.46 m COM distance, 0.52 m pivot-to-head-centre distance and 35 mm spherical steel-head radius. The head swings inside the bell, contacts the true inner wall through the inner-wall Fourier modal fields, and transfers force to the modes through a conservative Hertz law. The file is mono PCM16, 48 kHz, 6 seconds, peak-normalized to 0.95. The impact starts at approximately 75.7 ms; contact lasts approximately 1.02 ms, with a peak model force of 22.45 kN and conservative energy drift of 4.61e-5.

The coefficient uses a locally flat wall approximation; the clapper values are illustrative, not measurements. The output is a directional model-based listening preview, not calibrated SPL, stereo, room acoustics or validated contact acoustics. Full parameter and assumption details are in `docs/INTERNAL_CLAPPER_PHYSICAL_MODEL.md` and `audio/bell_clapper_metadata.txt`. Regenerate with `generate_clapper_audio.cmd`.
